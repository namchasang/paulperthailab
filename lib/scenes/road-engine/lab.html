Road Engine Lab
Real 3D roads from a few points (smooth height profiles, 6% max grade; T-junctions and crossroads with rounded kerbed corners, roundabouts where four or more meet with an arterial; every piece joined): arterial, streets, lanes and a highway bridge, with junctions, kerbs, footpaths, zebra crossings and traffic that keeps left. Detail layers: patches, cracks, manholes, lane arrows, parking bays, give-way/stop/speed signs, working traffic lights, cat's eyes, gutter leaves, skid marks and night headlights.
