# Nara video clips (Reels / TikTok): tools, prompts, lessons

## Tool (Nov 2026)
- Google Flow (labs.google/flow), model **Omni 1.2 Flash** (Omni 1.1 Flash, Veo 3.1 Fast and Veo 3.1 Quality retire on 5 Dec 2026). Veo 3.1 Lite = cheap tests.
- Settings: 9:16, x1 (not x2), 720p or higher for real posts. Free daily credits, watermark on the free plan.
- Best method: make an approved still in Gemini first, then use it as the FIRST FRAME (+ button) and describe only the motion, sound and timing.
- Kling: still has free daily credits (about 66/day); used for Nara Studio Lip Sync.

## Lessons
1. Selfie clips: one hand holds the phone the whole time, the other hand can do only one thing. If she holds something, write "her left hand holds the cone firmly the whole video, it never leaves her hand, no waving or other gestures with that hand". (Ice cream floated in the air when she waved with the cone hand.)
2. Signalised crossings in Australia are two parallel white lines, not zebra stripes: write "a signalised pedestrian crossing marked only by two parallel white lines, no zebra stripes".
3. Text-only prompts guess the place. For an exact location, use a real photo: clean it, put Nara in as a still, then animate.
4. Menu boards and signs garble in video: "the background is softly out of focus and stays still, no new text".
5. Give a second-by-second timeline (0-2 s, 2-4 s ...); a fixed turn time like "at exactly 5 seconds" works.
6. Sound line at the end: name each sound, then "no music, no other speech, no text on screen, no logos".
